# Script: Many small parts in, one product out

This file owns the scenes, their timing and the text of every intertitle. How each
scene looks is in [visual-descriptions.md](visual-descriptions.md), under the same
scene ID. The audience, format and other decisions are in the
[film brief](../README.md).

## The core image

A factory floor seen from above, with the flow running from left to right.

- **Left:** Many small boxes arrive on several automated belts. This is *shift left*:
  the parts are checked early, and this part has been automated for a long time.
- **Right:** A loading dock ships one large, finished box to the users. This is the
  release, and we have been able to do it for a long time.
- **Middle:** The belts end. People run and carry boxes between the belts. The boxes
  arrive at random times and in varying quality, so they are hard to put together.
- **Above:** The cloud, with servers and AI robots. Long cables run down to every
  station in the factory.

## Three zoom levels

The film moves between three levels with the same smooth camera move, so the viewer
always knows where they are:

1. **Overview:** The whole factory and the cloud.
2. **Section:** The left, the middle or the right.
3. **Detail:** A single box, gate or cable.

## Roles

Each role wears its own color, defined in
[visual-descriptions.md](visual-descriptions.md#style-block).

- **Users** receive the finished box.
- **Builders** build belts, gates and machines.
- **IT infrastructure** pulls cables and connects to the cloud.
- **Carriers** move boxes by hand in the middle. During the film they become Builders.
